🛑The problem

One of the biggest worries when moving to Spain is finding somewhere to live. Many people face:

  • ❌Trouble renting without an employment contract
  • ❌Rental scams aimed at people abroad
  • ❌Legal barriers and no clear information
  • ❌Landlords turning them down for having no history in Spain

For families, companies or parents sending their kids to study, this uncertainty can turn the dream of moving into a source of anxiety.

✅How we help

At Migración con Ayuda we offer personalized housing support with a legal and human approach. We help you:

  • ✅Search, filter and assess safe rental options
  • ✅Understand the real requirements for your immigration status
  • ✅Talk to landlords or agents while you're still abroad
  • ✅Review contracts so you don't sign anything that hurts you
  • ✅Get your housing ready as part of your arraigo or immigration process

❓Questions? We've got answers
Can I use the service if I'm not in Spain yet?

Yes. We support you from your home country so you have a solid option before you arrive.

Do you help with empadronamiento (address registration)?

Yes. We explain how it works and how it can help your arraigo or residence process.

Can I use this address for immigration procedures?

We advise you based on your visa or procedure and help you meet the legal requirements.

Does the service include property viewings?

If you're abroad, we help you arrange them with local agents. We also review contracts and terms before you sign.
