🛑The problem

Thousands of young players dream of going pro. Many get lost in academies with no backing, empty promises or programs with no real link to the football world. Parents, from far away, wonder:

  • ❌Is this a serious academy or just marketing?
  • ❌Will my child be safe and well looked after in another country?
  • ❌Will this really help them reach professional football?
  • ❌How do the visa, residence and school studies work?

With so many options, they end up losing time, money and real opportunities.

❓Questions? We've got answers
From what age can they join?

From 6 to 21 years old, depending on the program and training level.

What visa does my child need?

Usually a study visa with a sports component. We advise and support you through the whole process.

Does it include housing and safety for minors?

Yes. The selected academies offer boarding or supervised housing, plus health insurance.

Is it only for advanced players?

No. There are grassroots development programs and programs for pre-professional talent.
