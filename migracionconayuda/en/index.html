👩‍⚖️About me

Lawyer and Founder of Migración con Ayuda.

I'm Ana Elena Cordido, an immigration lawyer, certified migration coach and the founder of Migración con Ayuda. For more than a decade I've helped individuals, families, professionals and companies turn their move into a safe, strategic and legally solid reality.

I believe moving abroad should be a planned journey that combines:

  • 🔹Up-to-date knowledge of Spanish and European law
  • 🔹Practical strategies tailored to each story
  • 🔹Emotional support that puts the person first
