🛑The problem

Moving abroad means rebuilding your life from scratch: a new home, new habits and new financial decisions. Most migrants arrive without knowing:

  • ❌How to protect their savings in Spain
  • ❌What investment options exist for non-residents or new residents
  • ❌How to get insurance, retirement plans or bank products with no credit history
  • ❌How to grow financially in a legal, strategic way

Without clarity, many make poor decisions, lose money or trust offers with nothing behind them.

✅How we help

At Migración con Ayuda we give you access to financial products suited to your immigration status, with professional, safe and fully personalized guidance.

  • ✅We help you understand your legal and banking options as a migrant or resident
  • ✅We design a financial plan with you based on your income, risk and goals
  • ✅We connect you with real, secure investment products
  • ✅We show you how to protect your capital and plan long term, even if you've just arrived
